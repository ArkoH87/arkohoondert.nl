How it works

Every section follows the same order: the rule in plain language, an example, what it means for you, the realistic alternatives side by side, and what to watch out for.

You follow two fictional households through the guide: Priya and Tom, who moved to Amsterdam in 2014 with a pension in the United Kingdom, and Lars, who lives with his Dutch partner and is not married.

Every chapter ends with questions for yourself. They have no right answer. They help you see what you actually want before you decide what to arrange. Know someone who should read it? Send it on.
