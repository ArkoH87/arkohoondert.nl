About me

I am a financial life planner. I start with your life, not your money: what you want it to look like, and what happens if things go differently.

I have spent fifteen years raising money for charity, and was involved in raising over 250 million euros. Coming from that sector, I am radically transparent. You know the price before we begin.

I don’t sell financial products and I don’t take commission, so I can also tell you when not to do something.

Questions people ask first

Is everything in English?

Yes. The conversations, the plan and the reference document. Dutch terms are explained the first time they appear.

Do you give tax advice?

No. I show what the rules mean for your choices and prepare your conversation with a tax adviser who knows both countries.

Do you sell financial products?

No. When you need a mortgage, insurance or an investment, I refer you to an adviser with a licence.

Can we meet online?

Yes, every conversation can take place by video. If you would rather meet in person, we can arrange that too.

How do I pay?

For a review or plan you receive two invoices: half at the start and half on delivery. All prices include VAT.

What else do you do?

In Dutch, a lot more: wills, giving to charity, big decisions and yearly support. Read my Dutch site in English through the link at the bottom of this page.
