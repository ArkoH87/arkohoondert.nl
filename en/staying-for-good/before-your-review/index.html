Before your review

Thank you for booking your review. To spend our two hours on choices rather than facts, I ask you to fill in a short questionnaire first. It takes about half an hour.

Download the questionnaire (PDF)↓
01Fill it inType your answers on your screen in Adobe Acrobat Reader, Preview on a Mac or your browser, and save the file. Or print it. Living together? Fill it in once, together.
02Send it backEmail the saved form to arko@arkohoondert.nl at least three working days before your review. That gives me time to prepare.
03Have your papers at handThe last page lists the papers to have ready: pension statements, your marriage or partnership papers, your will. Bring them to the review or have them open on your screen. Please do not email them.

Reviews are booked at least ten days ahead, so there is time for this. Not sure about a question? Leave it open or write “not sure”: finding out is part of the review. Need to change the date? Email me. I use your answers only to prepare your review; see my privacy statement.
