What is in the plan

Everything a life plan covers

Every life plan I make answers four questions. They run through the guide too.

RouteWhere are you heading?Your life goals first, in your own words. Then the numbers.
RestDoes it hold in all four lives?A long life, a short life, a life that’s shitty, and a separated life. Each one calculated, with what is still missing in your safety net.
RhythmDoes it fit your month and your year?A plan only works if it fits the life you already live, without willpower.
RoomWhat is enough?Enough in an amount and a year. And what you no longer need to put off.

Plus six deep dives for internationals

On top of that, we go deeper into six topics that only come up when you came from somewhere else. Most of it was arranged for a stay, not for a life.

I plan from the Dutch side: Dutch rules, your Dutch pension, your life here. Where another country’s rules apply, I map what you have there and prepare your questions for an adviser who knows that country. I do not advise on foreign law or foreign tax.

01Your AOW gap and your pensionsEvery year you did not live here is missing from your Dutch state pension. Together with what you built up at home, that sets what you will really have. We put it all in one overview.
02Your marriage and your propertyWhere and how you married can change what is yours, what is shared, and what happens if you separate. You find out which questions to put to a notary.
03Your will and your childrenA will from home, a Dutch will, or both. And who looks after your children if you cannot.
04If you cannot decide for yourselfA lasting power of attorney for when illness or an accident takes the decisions out of your hands.
05Rent, buy, or keep both doors openBuying ties you down. Renting keeps you free. What fits depends on how sure you are that you are staying.
06If you leave after allWhat leaving would mean for your Dutch pension, your home and your plan, worked out before you need to know.

The Staying for good plan

What happens, step by step.

A few conversations, planned together. The planning and the fixed price are in the engagement letter before you decide anything.

Step 1The introductionTwenty minutes, free, by video. You tell me what is going on. You hear the fixed price for your situation, and whether a plan is worth it for you.
Step 2The inventoryWith your partner, as long as it takes. First what you want your life here to look like. Then your income, savings, pensions and home, here and abroad, and what is already arranged in each country. In between you gather your papers: pension statements, your will, your marriage or partnership papers.
Step 3The vision conversationI show you where you stand in each of the four lives. Together we make the choices: what is enough, what can happen now, and what you still need to arrange.
Step 4Your plan on paperRead by a second, certified planner before you get it. You decide and carry it out. Where you need a notary, tax adviser or licensed adviser, you know exactly what to ask them.
Step 5The follow-upTwo weeks after delivery we go through your questions and see how the first steps are going.

What you get: three documents

One sheetYour life mapWhere you stand in the four lives. Green is fine, orange needs attention, red is not arranged yet. With your next three steps.
